About this simulation

This independent Alexa-style experience uses Deepgram voice and Amazon Bedrock. Conversation supplies simulated household and camera context; it does not authenticate identity or observe a real camera. HomeBound MCP, AgentSafe, and Decionis govern every device request. Device states are execution snapshots. Approval takes place in Decionis Presence.

The microphone stays connected while enabled. Speech is sent after a pause, and listening resumes after each reply. Silence stays in your browser. The listener pauses while Alexa speaks so her reply does not become another request. Mute turns the microphone off.
