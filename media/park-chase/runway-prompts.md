## Continuity reference

Create an original cinematic 3D family-comedy cartoon with rounded expressive
characters, tactile fur, soft shadows and warm light. Keep the same characters,
wardrobe and proportions in every shot. The rabbit has cream fur, pink inner
ears, large expressive eyes and a playful face. The bulldog is stocky and
cinnamon-colored, with a cream muzzle and blaze, floppy ears, a blue collar and
expressive jowls. The rabbit is visibly smaller than the dog. Both are friendly
cartoon animals. The parents wear a saffron sweater and teal cardigan; their two
children wear a plum hoodie and a golden-yellow top. Use the same couple in the
park and the living room. No existing cartoon characters, logos, watermarks or
embedded words. The physical comedy is harmless and humorous.

The following six story sequences guided the 16:9, 60-second production.
Character and scene references supplied continuity. Generation used supported
five- and ten-second shots, splitting the train, fall, children, and rabbit
reactions into readable beats. The final edit retains the rabbit's mocking look.

## 01 - An ordinary afternoon

A sunlit, bustling city park inspired by Central Park. A couple sits close
together on a bench beneath a broad leafy tree. Children play on the lawn and
run past in the middle distance. A coral-colored local train rolls gently into
a small park-side station behind them. Wide cinematic establishing shot with a
slow camera move past foliage. Wind ruffles leaves and the couple's clothes.
The scene is happy and full of life. End with attention moving toward the train.

## 02 - An unexpected arrival

The same local train has stopped. Its doors open and several passengers descend
the steps onto the platform, carrying everyday bags. Cut back to the same park
tree: the cream rabbit wobbles on a low branch, ears windmilling, then tumbles
into a soft mound of leaves beside the cinnamon bulldog. It bounces up unharmed.
The startled dog raises its brows, then realizes the rabbit is already darting
away. Clear, readable physical action and a playful pause before the chase.

## 03 - Absolutely no brakes

The same cream rabbit bounds along a winding park path, pursued by the stocky
cinnamon bulldog with the blue collar. The rabbit makes nimble little turns;
the dog oversteers, paws scrambling, jowls and ears flapping. Wind swirls fallen
leaves around them. Children and passengers remain safely in the background,
watching the harmless commotion with amused surprise. Low tracking camera at
the animals' height, alternating with a readable wide shot. Frenetic, funny,
elastic movement with believable 3D weight.

## 04 - The windy shortcut

Continue the same chase through gusting leaves and past the station. The rabbit
slips around a corner while the bulldog skids wide and corrects himself. A
whip-pan follows them toward an open house doorway. Transition to a large,
cozy living room: amber lamps, a soft rug, teal sofa, bookcases, a glowing TV,
and an adjacent wooden dining table. The same parents and their two children
are comfortably watching TV and sharing bowls of popcorn, unaware of the
approaching chase. Finish with the rabbit appearing at the doorway.

## 05 - Movie night meets mayhem

In the same warmly lit living room, the family watches the TV and eats popcorn.
The cream rabbit darts past the sofa and slips beneath the dining table. The
stocky bulldog charges through the doorway after it, spots the family and
table, and braces all four paws. His body leans back as he slides to a dramatic
car-like hard stop on the rug, with a little comic dust puff and elastic jowls.
He stops safely without hitting anyone or anything. Time the main braking gag
near the end of the sequence; the children begin to react.

## 06 - The rabbit gets the last laugh

Both children jump up from the sofa in exaggerated surprise, laughing as
popcorn flies up and gently rains down. The parents react with amused disbelief.
The bulldog stands panting, shoulders pumping, tongue hanging out, completely
baffled. Cut to a low shot under the dining table: the cream rabbit peeks around
a table leg and gives the panting dog a cheeky sideways look, a tiny smug smile
and an ear flick. Hold this comic relationship clearly in the final frame.
Warm, cozy, humorous ending; no chasing or harm to the children.

background, a gentle train arrival and door chime, a falling slide whistle and
soft boing, rapidly pattering paws, wind and leaf whooshes, a clearly audible
comic tire-screech at the bulldog's stop, scattering popcorn, brief surprised
child reactions, and the dog's panting. Match the sound events to the selected
video edit. No copyrighted music or recognizable character voices. The brake
